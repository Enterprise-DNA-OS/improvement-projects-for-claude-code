# Evidence checks and their sources

Checked 14 November 2026. This is a review aid for internal improvement records. It does not certify a management system, approve a safety change or make a statutory filing.

| Rule | Check in this system | Source and scope |
|---|---|---|
| CI-DMAIC | Current define, measure, analyze, improve and control reviews; a baseline before measurement review; observations before improve; target attainment, closed actions, current passing controls and reviewed benefits before closure | [ASQ DMAIC](https://asq.org/quality-resources/dmaic). The five phases are the published method. These exact blocking conditions are our configurable operating policy, not statutory requirements. |
| CI-LOCAL | Missing owners, overdue projects and actions, 30-day stale reviews, unreviewed or stale benefit evidence, off-target measurements, failed or due controls | Internal operating policy. The 30-day review interval and separate finance reviewer are local controls. No claim that ASQ mandates these thresholds. |
| NZ-IPP9 | A project marked as containing personal information has a retention review date and it is not overdue | [NZ Privacy Commissioner, Principle 9](https://www.privacy.org.nz/privacy-principles/9/). Keep personal information only as long as a lawful purpose needs it. The date is chosen by the business, not a statutory fixed period. The check prompts review; it does not delete information. |

A passing check means the recorded fields passed these checks. Evidence references are entered by people and not fetched or authenticated. A separate reviewer name supports review discipline, not identity verification. Restrict write access to trusted operators. Database administrators can change records and history.

For Australian personal information or sector rules, add the applicable retention and approval policy before use. Never infer that this demo covers all legal obligations. Safety, food, product and environmental change approvals remain in the business's authorised systems.
