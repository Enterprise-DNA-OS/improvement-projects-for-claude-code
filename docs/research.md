# Selection and sources

Minitab Engage is the target for a new continuous-improvement family. It covers DMAIC project reviews, benefit evidence and sustain checks. The existing innovation build manages intake; strategy execution links goals; statistical process analysis analyses measurements. This build manages the evidence trail from problem to sustained change.

Scores in order: annual bill 3, data-shaped 5, buyer fit 2, search demand 2, one-session buildable 4, import path 5. Total 21/30. KaiNexus scored 2/5/2/2/4/4 = 19; Uptempo scored 2/5/2/2/3/4 = 18. These are analyst judgments. Search volume and an EDNA customer purchase were not verified. The local customer and CRM snapshots contained no named Engage buyer.

The vendor-hosted [GSA price schedule](https://www.minitab.com/content/dam/www/en/uploadedfiles/documents/pricing/GSACONTRACTPRICELIST.pdf) lists annual Engage licences for 10 to 24 users at USD 1,430.23 each. Fifteen such users give a historical USD 21,453.45 annual scenario. This is a US government schedule, not current NZ/AU commercial pricing or a customer invoice. The [current buying page](https://www.minitab.com/en-us/try-buy/) directs Engage buyers to sales. The comparison page uses no price figure.

[Minitab dashboard documentation](https://support.minitab.com/en-us/engage/getting-started/view-the-dashboard/) describes project status, phase, metrics and custom reports. We do not claim its reporting cannot answer a particular question. Our ten questions demonstrate checks this base answers from its own evidence rules.

[KaiNexus pricing](https://www.kainexus.com/continuous-improvement-software/kainexus/kainexus-pricing) describes custom prices but no current figure. [Uptempo financial management](https://docs.uptempo.io/get-started-financial.html) concerns marketing budget management; it was a weaker candidate for this run.

Sources reopened 14 November 2026. The synthetic demonstration names, records and amounts are not customer evidence or promised savings.
